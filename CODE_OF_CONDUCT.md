# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
Everyone participating in this project is welcome. We pledge to keep this community respectful, welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, patient and constructive.
- Assume good intent; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give credit and help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or ban participants who violate this code. Report problems privately to the maintainer via their GitHub profile.
